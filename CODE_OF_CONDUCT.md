# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the project:

* being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
* giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
* focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
* crediting other people's work, including AI assistance, as described in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* insults, personal or political attacks, and dismissive comments about a device, vendor or user;
* publishing someone's private information without permission;
* harassment in any form, public or private.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
Report problems to the maintainer listed in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`; reports are handled confidentially.
